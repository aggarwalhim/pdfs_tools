Tablet Weight Variation and Disintegration Log

Enter tablet weights or disintegration times, get pass or fail instantly, and print a log sheet for your lab record.

Weight variation limits used here: average weight 80 mg or less ±10%, more than 80 and less than 250 mg ±7.5%, 250 mg or more ±5%. The batch passes if not more than 2 tablets are outside the limit and none is outside twice the limit. Limits and disintegration times differ between IP, BP and USP and between tablet types. Check the pharmacopoeia your college follows and edit the limit if needed.
