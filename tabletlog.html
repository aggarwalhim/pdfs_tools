Tablet Weight Variation and Disintegration Log

Enter tablet weights or disintegration times and get pass or fail with a printable lab log sheet.

Limits used: average up to 80 mg → ±10%, 80 mg to under 250 mg → ±7.5%, 250 mg or more → ±5%. Pass if not more than 2 tablets are outside the limit and none is outside twice the limit. Check your current pharmacopoeia or lab manual.

If 1 or 2 tablets fail, repeat with 12 more tablets. The batch passes if at least 16 of the 18 tablets disintegrate within the limit. Limits are common values, so check your pharmacopoeia.
