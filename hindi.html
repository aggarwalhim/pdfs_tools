Hindi Typing (Hinglish to Hindi)

Type in English letters, get Hindi text. Free, no sign-up.

Typing tips
• Long sounds: aa=आ/ा, ee=ई/ी, oo=ऊ/ू. Example: seeta → सीता
• M = ं (bindu), ~ = ँ (chandrabindu), H = ः
• Capital letters: T D N S R give ट ड ण ष ड़ (small t d n s r give त द न स र)
• chh=छ, kh gh th dh ph bh = ख घ थ ध फ भ, gy=ज्ञ, ksh=क्ष
• Two consonants together make a joined letter: pyaar → प्यार, namaste → नमस्ते
