# tool4all_U

Free tools for students. Everything runs in your browser, and your files never leave your device.

## Tools
- PDF: merge, split, rotate
- Photos: exam photo and signature resizer, compress and convert, text on photo, colour and gradient maker
- Everyday: Hindi typing, graph maker, unit converter, stopwatch and timer, random picker, pincode and IFSC search
- Science and pharmacy: periodic table, molecular weight calculator, drug search and lab reagents, Rx abbreviations quiz, tablet weight variation and disintegration log, lab safety signs chart
- Pharmacy study sections: pharmacognosy crude drugs, pathophysiology of common diseases, drug reference (mechanism, side effects, interactions, half-life)

## Notes
- Drug and reagent lists are for learning only. Check a standard reference (IP, BNF, CIMS) before relying on them.
- Pincode and IFSC search use free public services and need internet.
