Molecular Weight Calculator

Type a chemical formula to get its molecular weight, and find how many grams to weigh for a solution.

-

Write capital then small letters (Na, Cl, Mg). For hydrates use a dot or ·, like CuSO4.5H2O. Brackets are allowed, like Ca(OH)2.

-

Grams to weigh = Molarity × Volume (L) × Molecular weight ÷ (Purity ÷ 100). Dissolve in some solvent, then make up to the final volume in a volumetric flask.
